Resting Heart Rate (RHR) is the number of heartbeats per minute when a person is at rest, usually measured when they are sitting or lying down.

A healthy RHR ranges from 60-100 beats per minute. A low RHR can indicate good cardiovascular fitness, while a high RHR can indicate an increased risk of heart disease.

Factors that affect RHR include age, physical activity, stress, and certain medical conditions.

Regular monitoring of RHR can provide insight into one's overall health.
