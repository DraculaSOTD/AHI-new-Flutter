A depression risk is a measure of depression severity. It can be based on self-reported symptoms, behavioral observations, or physiological measures.

Overall, a depression risk plays a crucial role in improving the accuracy of a depression diagnosis and ensures effective treatment for individuals with depression.

What is Depression?
Depression is a mental health disorder characterized by persistent feelings of sadness, hopelessness, and loss of interest in activities that were previously enjoyed. It is a common and serious medical illness that negatively affects how you feel, the way you think and how you act.
