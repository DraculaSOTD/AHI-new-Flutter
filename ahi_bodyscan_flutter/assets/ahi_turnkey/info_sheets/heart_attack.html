A heart attack is caused by a blockage in the blood flow to the heart, usually due to the build-up of plaque in the coronary arteries. This can be due to factors such as:

  • Atherosclerosis (hardening of the arteries)
  • High blood pressure
  • Smoking
  • Diabetes
  • High cholesterol
  • Family history
  • Physical inactivity
  • Obesity
  • Stress

Symptoms may include chest pain, shortness of breath, nausea, and light-headedness.
