Disclaimer and pre-scan text: 
Taking A Face Scan
This face scan will collect comprehensive vital signs including heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, and stress levels.The scan includes three consecutive measurements separated by one-minute breaks. The results are averaged to ensure accuracy. Please stay seated, minimize movement, avoid talking, and have a plain background to increase the accuracy of the results. 
- Even lighting to face
- Remove eyewear & masks
- Position face in outline to start
- Follow on-screen guide
- Aim for 5 stars for great results
We use the camera to scan your face, collecting small data packets for calculating health metrics. Your image is not used, stored, or transmitted. When prompted, tap 'Allow' to start the scan.
