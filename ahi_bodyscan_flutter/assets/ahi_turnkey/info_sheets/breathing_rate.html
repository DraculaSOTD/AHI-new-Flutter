Breathing rate, also known as respiratory rate, is a measure of the number of breaths a person takes per minute.

A normal breathing rate for an adult at rest is 12 to 16 breaths per minute. Factors such as age, activity level, and medical conditions can affect breathing rate.

Maintaining a healthy breathing rate can involve practicing good respiratory hygiene, such as avoiding exposure to airborne irritants, and engaging in regular physical activity.

Regular monitoring of breathing rate can also help detect respiratory problems early, allowing for prompt treatment and better outcomes.
