An anxiety risk is a measure of anxiety levels or severity. It can be based on various methods, such as self-reported symptoms, behavioral observations, or physiological measures.

Overall, the purpose of an anxiety risk is to provide a quantitative measure of anxiety levels, which can be used to inform the diagnosis and treatment of anxiety disorders and to monitor treatment outcomes.

What is Anxiety?
Anxiety is a feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease about something with an uncertain outcome. It is a normal and often healthy emotion, but when it becomes chronic and excessive, it can interfere with daily activities and quality of life.
