Heart Rate Recovery (HRR) is the decrease in heart rate in the first minute after stopping exercise.

It is an indicator of cardiovascular fitness and can predict the risk of heart disease and mortality. A faster HRR, meaning a greater decrease in heart rate in the first minute after exercise, is generally considered a sign of good cardiovascular health. A slower HRR, on the other hand, may indicate an increased risk of heart disease or other health problems.

HRR is measured by taking the pulse immediately after exercising and then at 1 minute and 2 minutes after stopping. It is important to note that HRR may be influenced by various factors such as age, medication use, and underlying medical conditions.
