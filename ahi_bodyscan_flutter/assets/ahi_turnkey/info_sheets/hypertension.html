Hypertension, or high blood pressure, is a medical condition in which the force of blood against the walls of the arteries is consistently elevated.

Hypertension can lead to serious health complications, including heart disease, stroke, kidney failure, and vision loss.

Hypertension can be caused by lifestyle factors such as an unhealthy diet, lack of physical activity, stress, and smoking.

It is important to work with a healthcare provider to develop an individualized plan for preventing and managing hypertension.
