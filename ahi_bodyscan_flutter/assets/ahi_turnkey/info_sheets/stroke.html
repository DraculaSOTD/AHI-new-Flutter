A stroke is a serious medical condition that occurs when the blood supply to the brain is disrupted, causing brain cells to die.

This can lead to temporary or permanent disability, including paralysis, speech problems, and memory loss.

Some common risk factors for stroke include high blood pressure, smoking, obesity, physical inactivity, and a history of heart disease.

Early recognition of the signs of stroke and prompt treatment can greatly improve outcomes and reduce the risk of serious complications.

The World Health Organization reports that high blood pressure is a major contributing factor to stroke and is involved in more than 12.7 million strokes worldwide.
